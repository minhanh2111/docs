Welcome to Luklak. We believe software should adapt to your business, not the other way around. Unlike traditional tools that force you into rigid, pre-built boxes, Luklak empowers you with a **3-Layer Unified Architecture**. 

Think of this as more than just a platform—it’s a living organism where data, logic, and interface work in perfect harmony. Together, let’s explore how to bring your vision to life.

---

## Part 1: The Foundation (Layer 1 — Data & Objects)

<Note>**Our Goal:** To define the "DNA" of your business data so everything stays connected.</Note>

Every great system starts with a strong foundation. In Luklak, we begin by defining exactly what you want to track.

### 1. The Universal Object
The **Universal Object** is the heartbeat of Luklak. Whether you are managing a "Task," a "High-Value Lead," or a "Legal Contract," the system treats them all with the same underlying intelligence. 

*   **Why this empowers you:** Because every piece of data shares a common "language," your departments can finally collaborate without the friction of complex integrations. Information flows where it's needed, when it's needed.

### 2. Functions & Object Types: Your Blueprint
*   **Functions (The Ecosystem):** Think of a Function as the "Master Blueprint" for a specific part of your business, like a CRM or HR Hub.
*   **Object Types (The Specifics):** Inside that blueprint, you define the details—like "Standard Tasks" or "Project Milestones."

<Tip>
**A Pro Tip for Growth**
We recommend breaking complex processes into **Standard** and **Sub-object** types. This keeps your data clean and makes reporting on "parent-child" relationships effortless as you scale.
</Tip>

### 3. Universal Query Language (UQL)
UQL is your internal search engine. It allows you to instantly find and link data across any Function in your organization.

*   **The Visual Builder:** If you prefer a hands-on approach, use our drag-and-drop interface to build filters in seconds.
*   **Advanced Mode:** For those who love precision, UQL scripting handles even the most complex data relationships with ease.

## Part 2: The Brain (Layer 2 — Logic & Automation)

<Note>**Our Goal:** To automate the mundane so you can focus on the meaningful.</Note>

Once your data is set, we need to give it a brain. Using our **Trigger–Condition–Action** framework, you can teach Luklak how to handle work for you.

### 1. Bringing Your Workflow to Life
There are three primary ways to spark action in Luklak:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Background Rules" icon="sparkles">
    These work tirelessly behind the scenes, listening for updates or running on a schedule you define.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transition Rules" icon="route">
    These activate the moment an item moves from one stage to another (like shifting a project from "Planning" to "Live").
  </Card>
  <Card title="Action Buttons" icon="mouse-pointer">
    Empower your team with custom buttons that trigger specific automations right when they need them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
**Building for Reliability**
When designing "Action Chains," keep an eye on how rules interact. We want to ensure your automations run in a smooth sequence, avoiding loops where rules trigger each other indefinitely.
</Warning>

### 2. Smart Values
**Smart Values** make your system dynamic. Instead of simple static updates, you can ask Luklak to perform calculations or pull data on the fly.

> *Imagine this:* "Automatically calculate a project's total cost by multiplying `Team Hours` by the `Hourly Rate` stored in your HR Function."

## Part 3: The Experience (Layer 3 — UI & Interaction)

<Note>**Our Goal:** To design an interface that your team will actually love using.</Note>

This is where your work meets the world. Because the data and logic layers are already unified, the user experience feels consistent, intuitive, and polished.

### 1. Spaces: Where Your Vision Becomes Reality
If a Function is your blueprint, a **Space** is the finished building where your team works every day. It’s the live instance of your design, tailored for a specific team or project.

### 2. Workviews & Live Dashboards
*   **Workviews:** Give your team the right tool for the job—whether that’s a Kanban board for developers, a Calendar for marketing, or a Timeline for project managers.
*   **Unified Dashboards:** Get a bird's-eye view of your entire operation. These aren't just static charts; they are **"Live."** See a dip in performance? Click the chart to dive directly into the specific tasks and find a solution.

## Part 4: Your Path to Implementation

Building in Luklak is a rewarding journey. We suggest following these three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shape Your Vision">
    * Create your **Function**.
    * Define your **Object Types** and the fields that matter to you.
    * Map out the **Workflow** (the stages of your process).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the Intelligence">
    * Add **Automation Rules** to handle the heavy lifting.
    * Use **Smart Values** for real-time calculations.
    * Set up **Permissions** to ensure everyone has the right level of access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Empower Your Team">
    * Launch a **Space** for your users.
    * Customize **Workviews** to match their daily habits.
    * Build a **Dashboard** to celebrate wins and track progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>
